Show the talk. Load it on play.

A local poster until you press play, so nothing loads early.

6:20–7:10. Press play and let it run for the beat you came for. Leave the slide and it unloads.

Set data-youtube to the video id and data-start to the second to begin at. Before you press play the slide makes no request to YouTube. On http(s) the poster becomes a privacy-enhanced embed; opened from disk or offline it opens the watch page in a new tab instead, because YouTube refuses embeds that send no referrer. “Open on YouTube” is always there as a fallback.
