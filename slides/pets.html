The hook · meet the pet

Your pet has four moods.

  1. “Hi! I’ll be here all talk.”

    wave Cover and close: hello and goodbye.

  2. “Watch this. It’s live.”

    coding Demos and anything running for real.

  3. “Good question. Hmm…”

    thinking Questions, open problems and pauses.

  4. “That wasn’t meant to happen.”

    facepalm When the demo breaks. Laugh, then recover.

1:10–1:40. Introduce the pet once, so the audience knows what its moods mean later. Space brings in each mood.

Lineup: ol.pets > li holding p.pet-say, i.pet[data-pose], b for the pose and span for when to use it. .pet-say works beside any pet; --tail moves its tail. The template brings the pet images; the default is a drawn pixel robot.
