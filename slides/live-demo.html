The screenshot shows until you press L or Go live; then the frame loads data-src and fills the slide. Point data-src and the URL pill at your dev server, such as http://localhost:5173, and replace bucket.png with a 16:9 capture of the same screen. data-cap sets the countdown in seconds. While the frame has focus, keys go to the app: click Back instead of pressing L. Leaving the slide also goes back.
