A real page runs in the frame. Click it on stage, or let the room call the clicks.

10:00–10:40. “Five tokens, one back every second. Send a few… now burst. Two get through; the rest get a 429.”

The frame loads data-src when the slide opens and goes back to about:blank when you leave, so its timers and sound stop. To use your own, put one self-contained HTML file in assets/ and point data-src and the URL pill at it. Expand fills the slide; Reload starts it fresh. Clicks inside the frame keep the keyboard, so click the slide edge before pressing Space.
