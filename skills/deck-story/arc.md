# A worked arc

A 13-minute meetup talk about how to keep track of long agent runs. It had 19 main slides, then a bonus and four presenter extras. The titles alone tell the story.

| # | Title | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Many agents. Many hours. One page. | Cover: the promise in six words |
| 2 | My farewell film. Made with agents. | Hook: 13 seconds of a real clip, before anyone knows the speaker |
| 3 | Twenty-two hours. 842 million tokens. | Stakes: real numbers that count up |
| 4 | Speaker | One breath, after the hook |
| 5 | 38 subagents. Good luck keeping track. | The problem, felt: an animated graph that gets messier on Space |
| 6 | In the app: click, click, click. | The problem, felt again: a drawn app where a cursor clicks row after row |
| 7 | The best model for each job. Both labs. | Why it gets harder: many vendors and many models |
| 8 | One page. Not twenty chats. | The turn, in one line |
| 9 | Ask once. Watch six hours. | Demo: a simulated run, replayed in a minute |
| 10 | Every milestone brings a picture. | Demo: the same run as a short story |
| 11 | A keeper writes the story. | How it works, in three rules |
| 12 | My farewell film. The whole night. | Proof: the real page, with an optional live beat |
| 13 | What the page put in front of me. | Proof: the problems that it caught |
| 14 | Each judge liked its own lab's plan. | A surprise from the run, and a lesson |
| 15 | See the art. Steer it. | The human part: taste stays with you |
| 16 | The tech I survived. | Delight: a clip from the film |
| 17 | Steal this for your long runs. | Takeaway: one row per idea |
| 18 | Take the skill. Watch your next run. | The way in: a QR to the skill |
| 19 | Here's to the next first commit. | Close: the line that ends the film |
| 20 | Bonus: the whole film. | After the questions, only if there is time |

What makes it work:

- The hook is a real clip, and the numbers come before the speaker slide.
- Slides 5 to 7 make the pain real before slide 8 names the fix.
- Each demo is simulated, says so in its eyebrow, and runs on Space, so the speaker sets the pace.
- The proof includes what went wrong (slide 13) and a result nobody expected (slide 14).
- The extras answer the likely questions: how long it took, who did what, the limits, and where the work ran.
- `PRESENTER.md` says which slides to cut if the talk runs long.
